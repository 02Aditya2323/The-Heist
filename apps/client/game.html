How the city works

The one rule

Veyra City holds a fixed amount of money and the server never creates more. Everything you earn is moved to you from a prize pool, or taken from another operator. Your score is whatever you are holding at the end.

Earning

  1. Walk to a district and find a lit terminal.
  2. Press E to open it and accept a contract.
  3. Solve it, then submit your answer in the journal (J).

Every contract states its own answer format, with a worked example. Capitals and extra spaces are forgiven. A wrong answer costs no money, but there is a short cooldown between tries.

Keeping it

Earnings land in your Home, where other operators can burgle them. Money you deposit at the Bank is safe from burglary, but a successful bank raid can still take a share of it. Withdrawals are not instant.

Getting around

Press M for the city atlas. Districts are joined by connectors; stand on a travel gate and press E to move between them. Walking takes real time, so where you go is a real choice.

If you are stuck

Read NEXT MOVE in the bottom-left of the HUD: it always names the single most useful thing you can do right now. If it says to find a contract, open the atlas with M, pick a district, and walk to a terminal there. NEXUS notifications tell you when races and other timed events open.
